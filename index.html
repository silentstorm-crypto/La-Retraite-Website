Welcome

Slow down. The river's in no hurry either.

La Retraite is a small, private riverside retreat about 5 km west of Parys on Schoemansdrift Road. Terraced lawns run straight down to the Vaal, a big old tree gives shade by the water, and the only traffic is the odd raft drifting past.

Come for a weekend of fishing with the guys, a family getaway where the kids run barefoot, or a few quiet days to read, braai and watch the sun go down over the water. About an hour's drive from Johannesburg.

The experience

Days by the water

Whether you're here for the fish or just the view, the river is the heart of La Retraite.

Serious fishing

Deep water, very few snags and big carp, barbel and yellowfish. Easy landing spots along the bank, with lights at the water for night sessions.

Braai at the water's edge

Every cabin has its own braai, and there's a fire spot down by the river for long evenings under the stars.

Life on the Vaal

Watch the kayaks and rafts drift by, cool off in the pool in summer, or head into Parys for its cafés, antique shops and river adventures.

Where you'll stay

Comfortable cabins, fully self-catering

Spacious, well-kept log cabins, each with its own bathroom, kitchen and braai, set in a lush, landscaped garden.

Family cabins

Room for the whole group, ideal for families and fishing crews.

Couples' cabin

A cosy hideaway for two. Bring a book and switch off.

Fully equipped kitchens

Everything you need to cook your own meals, plus the on-site restaurant when you'd rather not.
